## Locations

`%appdata%\metro-maker4\cities\data` is for all maps, both modded and native.

## Map Format

Contains:

- building_tags.json.gz
- coastline.geojson.gz
- foundations.pmtiles
- roads.geojson.gz
- tiles.pmtiles
- buildings_index.bin.gz
- demand_data.json.gz
- ocean_depth_index.json.gz
- runways_taxiways.geojson.gz
